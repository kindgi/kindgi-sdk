Closing ends a Kindgi Cloud workspace: nothing in it runs again, and once
30 days are over it's deleted. To stop paying and keep the workspace and
its data, [stop paying](../plans-and-billing/#stop-paying) instead: nothing
in it runs once the month you've paid for is over.

## Before you close

Export what you want to keep:
[Export your data](../export-your-data/) shows how. An export
holds what's stored in the workspace, as files, except:

- **Secret values,** which can't be read back, by design.
- **Your packs' images and the operational logs.**

You can still export in the 30 days after the close.

## Ask us to close it

Write to contact@kindgi.com from the email address of one of the
workspace's admins, or of its
[billing contact](../plans-and-billing/#the-billing-contact), and name the
workspace. We confirm it within 24 hours. The console and the billing page
have no button for it.

## What closing does

When we close the workspace:

- **Nothing in it runs,** and nothing in it can go live.
- **A subscription ends that day.** Nothing more is billed, and what's been
  paid isn't refunded. An invoice that's already open is still owed.
- **Every admin and the billing contact get one email,** "_Your
  organization_'s Kindgi account is closed: export it by _date_", with the
  day the 30 days end.

For those 30 days the workspace can still be read: sign in as before, or
use its own API keys, and export what's stored in it.

## Once the 30 days are over

Within 7 days we delete the workspace, as section 9 of our
[data processing addendum](https://www.kindgi.com/legal/dpa) says: its
records, its secrets, and its packs' services and images. Every admin and
the billing contact get an email once it's deleted.

Backups age out within 30 days after the deletion. If we restore a backup
before then, we delete the workspace from it again.

## What's kept

We keep your account and billing records, and nothing of your workspace's
content. Our [privacy page](https://www.kindgi.com/privacy) says which
records, and for how long.

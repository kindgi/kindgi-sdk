Kindgi Cloud runs the Kindgi runtime for your organization at
platform.kindgi.com. You get a workspace, with its console and its API, and
run no server yourself.

During the preview, we admit organizations one by one. You sign up with the
link we sent you, and once we admit yours, we email you as soon as its
workspace is ready.

## What the preview includes

- **Building and testing are free.**
- **One live app, free until Kindgi 1.0:** one project in production, within
  the [limits](../limits/). We give 60 days' notice before any charge.
- **More than one live app:** write to contact@kindgi.com.
- **Support:** write to contact@kindgi.com, every day, weekends included. The
  first reply comes within 24 hours.

## Sign up

1. **Open the sign-up link we sent you.** The page, "Create your Kindgi
   workspace", says: "We admit accounts to the preview one by one. If we
   admit yours, we'll email you as soon as your workspace is ready."
2. **Give your name, your work email and your organization,** then select
   **Email me a link**.
3. **Open the link in the email,** "Your Kindgi sign-up link", within 30
   minutes, and select **Continue**. Nothing happens until you do, so a mail
   scanner that opens the link doesn't use it up. A link that was used
   already, or is more than 30 minutes old, shows "This link can't be used":
   sign up again for a new one.
4. **Fill in your organization's details:** its legal name, a billing contact
   and their email, its address, and its GST/HST or VAT number if it has
   one.
5. **Accept the terms and the data processing agreement** on your
   organization's behalf. The page links both, at the version you accept,
   and the list of who processes data for us. Then select **Create the
   workspace**.

The page then says "Your sign-up is saved", and an email with the same
subject says so too.

## While we look at your sign-up

- **If we can't admit you within 30 days,** we delete your sign-up.
- **To withdraw,** use the link in the email. We then don't create your
  workspace, and we delete your sign-up's details within 30 days, except the
  terms you accepted.
- **Signing up again** with the same address doesn't start a second sign-up:
  we email you that you've already signed up.

## When we admit you

You get an email whose subject names your organization ("Acme's Kindgi
workspace is ready"), with a link to sign in to the console with the address
you signed up with.

- **You're the workspace's first admin.**
- **The billing page,** for the workspace's admins, says your workspace is in
  the preview: "Your workspace is in the preview: hosting one live app is free
  until Kindgi 1.0, within the hosted limits. We give 60 days' notice before
  any charge. For more than one live app, contact us."

If we admit you while no workspace is free, you first get an email, "You're
on the list for Kindgi": we're adding capacity. The email above follows as
soon as your workspace is ready.

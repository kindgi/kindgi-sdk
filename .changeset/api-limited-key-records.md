On a runtime without an authorization store, a key or session limited to a project is refused, `403 key-project-mismatch` (`details.keyProjectId`, `details.projectId`), an act by a record's id that a store refuses it in another project, before anything is written: cancelling or resuming a run, or exporting its provenance; rescoring or cancelling a test run of a test set in another project; deciding an approval or signing its audit bundle; correcting a run, and changing or removing a correction (its author still removes their own), or changing one whose agent or flow is in another project; closing or unregistering a conversation, or closing one whose agent is only in another project; deleting an artifact; writing a deployment's secrets; pinning a suggested change, or adding it as Latest, whose agent or scope is in another project, and withdrawing one, or cancelling an Improve run, whose agent's new versions are made in another project; and removing a connection choice. A session is refused the same way on a workspace's approval or artifact, and a key or a session on a workspace's deployment's secrets or a suggested change's workspace- or org-wide pin, without `details.projectId`. Records in the key's own project, ids the runtime doesn't find, and reads pass as before. A runtime with a store is unchanged.

**A connection has a name, its `label`.** `POST /v1/connections` takes an optional `label`, the name to give the connection it makes: trimmed, 1 to 100 characters, with no control, format or invisible characters (such as bidi overrides, zero-width characters or line separators, and a variation selector only in an emoji), else `400 bad-input` with `details.field` `label`. When the connect finishes, a new connection takes that name, or else its account's, such as an email address, without those characters and cut to 100 characters. One owner's live connections to an app in an environment each have their own name, whatever the case, an emoji's variation selector or how accented letters are encoded: a name another of them has is refused at the connect (`409 connection-label-taken`), and a new connection whose name was taken in the meantime gets the first free number after it, such as `ana@acme.com 2`. A reconnect keeps the connection's name, so the one it asks for isn't checked. `PATCH /v1/connections/{connectionId}` (`client.connections.update(connectionId, { label })`) renames one: its owner or a workspace admin may, as for a disconnect, and anyone else gets `404 connection-not-found`. A taken name is `409 connection-label-taken`. A connection and a connect request carry `label` when they have one; a connection made before connections had names has none. `ConnectionBinding` gains `update`, and its errors `connection-label-taken`.

- `hasHiddenCharacters` is the one rule for these names: a site's display name and a console link's label now also refuse a lone surrogate and the characters that render as nothing (Unicode's default-ignorable ones, such as the Hangul fillers), and a variation selector anywhere but in an emoji. An emoji with its variation selector, such as ✉️ or 1️⃣, still passes. A deployment whose console link label holds one of the newly refused characters (a lone surrogate, a Hangul filler, a combining grapheme joiner, or a variation selector after a letter) doesn't start after the upgrade, and the error names the link: take the character out. `comparableName` is what two connection names compare by, and `fitName` makes a name from an account's label: its hidden characters removed, trimmed, and cut between the characters a person sees, so a flag or a keycap is never split.
- `@kindgi/connections-inmemory`: names connections the same way, and renames them with `update`. A seeded connection takes an optional `label`, and none by default.

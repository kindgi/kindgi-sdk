**A project's admins choose which company connection an agent or a flow uses for an app.** The company can hold several connections of one app, so a choice names one of them, for one agent or flow in a project, in this server's environment. `PUT /v1/connection-choices` (`client.connectionChoices.set({ projectId, agentId, connectionId })`, or `flowId` in place of `agentId`) makes it, for the connection's app. The connection must be a live company connection in this environment (working, or needing a reconnect), on a registration that allows the project: `GET /v1/connection-choices/candidates` (`client.connectionChoices.candidates`) lists them. A person's own connection is `400 bad-input`, since it's used for that person, and any other connection that can't be chosen is `404 connection-not-found`, the same answer as for an id that doesn't exist. An agent or a flow that isn't one of the project's is `404 agent-not-found` or `404 flow-not-found`. Choosing the connection already chosen answers that choice as it is. Choosing another replaces it in one step with a new choice at the next `version`, since a choice is never edited. `GET /v1/connection-choices?projectId=` (`client.connectionChoices.list`) lists the project's live choices, narrowed by `agentId`, `flowId` or `app`, and with `includeRemoved=true` the removed ones too, with `removedAt` and `removedBy`: a choice's history. The project's members may read them, and the candidates too: a member who isn't an admin sees each candidate's `connectionId` and `name` only. `DELETE /v1/connection-choices/{choiceId}` (`client.connectionChoices.remove`) removes one, and keeps it in that history; an unknown or removed id is `404 connection-choice-not-found`, a new code. Disconnecting a connection leaves its choices listed until someone sets or removes them. `ConnectionBinding` gains `listChoices`, `getChoice`, `setChoice`, `removeChoice` and `listCandidates`, and its errors `connection-choice-not-found`, `agent-not-found` and `flow-not-found`.

- `@kindgi/connections-inmemory`: keeps choices the same way. A removed choice stays, with `removedAt` and `removedBy`, and versions count per project, environment, agent or flow, and app. Its `inProject` option says whether an agent or a flow is one of a project's; without it, none is, and every choice is refused.
